ShellacFilters use framework from Airwindows (MIT licensed).

Declick and Dehum had been trained on various shelllac transfers, including those from [tangotunes.com](https://tangotunes.com) and [tangotimetravel.be](https://tangotimetravel.be)

Dehum was inspired by loud noise around 40Hz in several transfers, primarily in Cachirulo and Tablada by Anibal Troilo. TT transfers were compared with TTT which have no low frequency noise.

The idea of Declick came after a super clean transfer from original metal master disc was released: https://tangotimetravel.be/product/malvon/

Unlike AU and VST, specialized plugins (foobar2000, VirtualDJ, etc) are able to scan files entirely. The filters are also integrated in [EmbraceNG](https://github.com/shaforostoff/EmbraceNG)
and [Tango Display & Setlist Player](https://github.com/richardsladetdj-creator/TangoDisplay)
If you would like them to be integrated into your favourite player (and it is either open source, or supports plugins) - feel free to ask me: https://www.facebook.com/shaforostoff

Parametric EQ was inspired by the [article by El Espejero](https://elespejero.wordpress.com/2014/12/17/eq-4-tj-getting-the-best-sound-out-of-your-tango-records/)
